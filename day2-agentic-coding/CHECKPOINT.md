# Day 2 checkpoint

## What you demonstrate

**One reviewed, merged agent-assisted change, with a written review note.**

1. A branch with your change, and a diff you can walk someone through.
2. Tests that cover the behaviour you changed.
3. `review-note.md` — what you accepted, what you **rejected**, and what remains
   unverified.

The rejected section is the one that matters. A review note with nothing rejected
usually means the review did not happen. Rubber-stamping an agent's diff is the
failure mode this day exists to prevent.

## Also bring

- Your `baseline-estimate.md`, filled in on both sides
- A one-line answer to: *which part of today would have been faster by hand?*

## Team working agreement

Before you leave, your team drafts `team-working-agreement.md`: what you will and
will not let an agent do unreviewed in your repository. One page. You will revise
it on Day 4 once you have seen what goes wrong.
