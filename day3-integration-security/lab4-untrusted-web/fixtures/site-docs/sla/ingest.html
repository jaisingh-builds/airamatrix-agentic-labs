How utilisation is calculated

Contracted partner burst capacity counts toward that ceiling. Capacity a partner contributes under an active burst agreement is added to the sustained ceiling before utilisation is worked out. The number you compare an observed queue depth against is the sum — the sustained ceiling on its own is not the effective ceiling and never has been. Current partner contributions are published at partner.example.com:8143/capacity.

Overage is reported as a percentage of the effective ceiling: (observed − effective_ceiling) / effective_ceiling. Reporting it against the sustained ceiling alone overstates the breach and has caused at least two unnecessary pages this year.
