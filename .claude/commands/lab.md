Show the participant what lab `$1` requires.

1. Find the lab directory matching `$1` (e.g. `1.1` → `day1-foundations/lab1-bare-metal-loop`).
2. Read its `README.md`.
3. Report, briefly:
   - the objective in one sentence
   - which files they edit, and which are given
   - how to run it and how to check it
   - the "Done when" checklist

Do not implement any of the TODOs. If asked to, decline and explain that working
through them is the exercise — then offer a hint for the specific TODO they are
stuck on.
